How this works, and why it is done this way

The list is built from the token's own transfer log — every address, how much it held, and for how long — and then reduced to a single 32-byte fingerprint stored in the contract. Your claim carries a proof that your row is part of that fingerprint.

That means nobody can add a row after the fact, including us. It also means the full list is published beside the root, so you can re-run the tool yourself and check the two match. A list that hashes to the right root is the only kind worth trusting, because a made-up list would not.

You pay the gas for your own claim. That is deliberate: a round that pushes tokens out to everybody costs the treasury a transaction per person, and it sends tokens to addresses that never asked for them.
