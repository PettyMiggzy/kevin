I. The origin

Somebody clicked No.

In 2009, in a computer lab that smelled like hand sanitiser and warm plastic, a kid opened MS Paint to draw a famous red video-game character from memory. He had seen him once, on a cereal box, at an angle, through a car window.

He drew what he remembered: one eye bigger than the other, hair like a fire hydrant exploded, a mouth that is technically a triangle, legs with no knees and no intention of ever having knees, and a background bucket-filled yellow because yellow was first and the bell was about to ring.

VII. The launch

Four days in the auction.

Kevin launches through the kekfun.xyz launchpad and settles on Robinhood Chain. The auction phase runs for four days — everybody bids into the same window at the same price, no sniping, no presale list, no "team allocation" you find out about afterwards.

1,000,000,000Total supply · hard cap

That last one isn't a promise, a pledge, or a thing you have to take our word for. The split is written into the token by the factory contract the moment it's created and can never be changed by anybody, including us. Verify it yourself on Blockscout.

1

Raise · 4 days

10% of supply is released each day for four days. You deposit ETH into whichever day you like. Your share of that day's tokens is pro-rata to everyone else who deposited that day — so if bigger money shows up after you, your allocation shrinks. That's the mechanism, and it's the same for everybody.

2

No snipers, no presale, no list

It's a dutch auction (VRGDA), not a bonding curve race. There's no block-zero advantage to buy, no allowlist to be left off, and nobody got a better price because they knew somebody. Four days, one window, everybody in the same queue.

3

Settle · liquidity locks forever

When the window ends, anyone can trigger settlement — it isn't ours to withhold. The raised ETH has already been spent into the composition legs, and settle mints a full-range Uniswap V4 position per funded leg and locks the NFT in the LP locker. The principal cannot be withdrawn. Not by us, not by anyone, not ever.

4

Claim · 30-day vest

Your allocation unlocks linearly over 30 days from settle. Partial claims any time. Nobody — including whoever bought most — gets their bag in one lump on day one.

5

Then nothing is promised

No roadmap, no partnerships deck, no utility nobody asked for. A community being petty in public, on purpose. That's it. That's the product.

Mechanics above are the kekfun protocol's, on Robinhood Chain (id 4663) — not house rules Kevin invented, and not something anyone here can bend. Read them yourself in the kekfun docs before you send anything anywhere.

XI. Questions

Ask, and he will look left of you.

Is Kevin that one red video-game character?

No. That character is owned by a corporation with a legal department, a brand guide and a mandated shade of red. Kevin has vibes and a yellow void. He is legally, spiritually and anatomically his own guy, and this is the single most important fact about him.

Why does the art look like that?

Because the wonk is load-bearing. Straighten his eyes, shade his hair, give him knees, and what you get is worse — not worse-looking, worse. Less alive. A photograph of a joke. Everything here is drawn slightly wrong on purpose.

Why three pools?

KEK is the laugh, GME is the grudge, WETH is the gas. A joke on its own is noise, a grudge on its own is a complaint, and plumbing on its own is a bill. You need all three to get out of the void.

Robinhood Chain? Seriously?

Seriously. A coin built on a sixteen-year grudge, carrying a GME liquidity pool, settling on Robinhood's own chain. Rule three says never explain the grudge, so that's all you're getting. Nobody here is mad at anybody — this is just where the pool went, and it happens to be the funniest place it could have gone.

You're really burning your whole allocation?

That's the commitment. The protocol gives the creator 0% to begin with, so we bid in the auction like anyone else from a wallet we publish before it opens — then burn everything it receives, daily, across the 30-day vest, with hashes posted each time. Until those hashes exist it's just words on a page, and you should treat it that way. In four days you'll be able to check instead of trust.

Can the team rug the liquidity?

No, and not because we're nice. Settlement locks the LP positions in the protocol's locker contract with no withdrawal path for anybody — creator included. The creator can claim trading fees on those positions; the principal is gone forever the second it settles. Creator token allocation is 0%, written into the token at creation by the factory. Don't believe any of that — go read the contracts on Blockscout.

What if nobody bids on some days?

A day with zero deposits doesn't block anything. That day's tranche folds into locked LP instead of minting claims — a quiet window just makes the liquidity deeper. Being early on a slow day isn't a punishment.

Does Kevin actually play games?

He has been holding a controller since the 2013 GTA 6 trailer and has not put it down. There is no console in the void. This has not stopped him.

What's the roadmap?

There isn't one, and anybody who hands you a memecoin roadmap is handing you a slide. The receipts are the roadmap.

Be petty in public and post the receipt. Timestamped, real, and not punching down. Sizes and the payout wallet get posted in the group once the auction settles.
