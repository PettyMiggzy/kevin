## Why this is the narrative and not just a joke

Most memecoins pick a character and stop. The character here is an argument
about the chain it launched on.

Robinhood Chain exists to bring tokenised stocks and RWAs on-chain — the
serious end of crypto, built by the app that put a brokerage in everyone's
pocket. Every other token on it will reach for that seriousness.

Kevin goes the other way and lands somewhere truer: the actual Robinhood
user is not an institution. It is somebody on a break, on their phone, with
more conviction than capital. That person built this chain's user base. He
should have a mascot.

That is why the GME pool works. It is not a reference — it is the same
person, the same instinct, on the same app, a few years later.

## The receipts (what makes it defensible)

Every line here is checkable in about ninety seconds. Say these plainly; do
not dress them up.

- **Zero creator allocation.** Not "zero for now" — the launchpad factory
  has no field for it.
- **No mint function. No owner. No pause. No blacklist.** All 28 functions in
  the token's implementation were read; none of them can change supply or
  freeze a wallet. There is nothing to renounce because there was never an
  owner.
- **Fixed supply**, 1,000,000,000, with ~40% held in the pad's vesting escrow
  until 7 Oct 2026 and the address published.
- **LP fees are published with transaction hashes** at iamkevin.lol/#fees.
  Not a claimed number — the actual transfers, linked to the explorer.
- **Three pools**: WETH, KEK, GME.
- **It is all built.** A site, a documentation page, a Telegram bot in his
  voice, four playable games, 120 hand-drawn stickers and emoji, and four
  contracts with 229 passing tests. The contracts are **not deployed** and
  the site says so.

## What NOT to claim

- Any date for anything unshipped.
- The GME round. The snapshot is taken and the list is published at /claim.
  The distributor is not deployed and there is no date. Its window has already
  closed, so nobody can buy their way onto the list.
- That there is a floor, a defended price, or downside protection.
- Utility. There isn't any. That is not a weakness of the pitch; pretending
  otherwise is.
