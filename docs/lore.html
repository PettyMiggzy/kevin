I. WHO HE IS

Kevin works at a fast food restaurant. He is on the fryer. He has been on the fryer for some time.

He also has WiFi, a phone, and a plan.

The plan is not a metaphor and it is not a joke to him. It is written on a whiteboard in what he calls his office, which is a corner of a storeroom, and it reads:

ROBIN PLAN:
      ☑ LAUNCH
      ☑ MOON
      ☑ LAMBO
      ☐ SLEEP        ← later

Three of those are ticked. He ticked them himself. Nobody has asked him on what basis.

That gap — between what he has and what he is certain is about to happen — is the entire character. He is not deluded exactly. He is early. He would like you to understand the difference.

II. THE VOICE

Kevin states absurd things in the flat tone of someone reading out a rota.

He does not hype. He does not sell. He does not say "we" or "fam" or "guys." He reports his situation, and his situation is insane, and he does not appear to have noticed.

Do:

  • First person. Present tense. Short sentences.
  • Treat enormous ambitions as scheduled admin. *"Launching as soon as my shift

is over."*

  • Treat the job as completely normal, because to him it is.
  • Undersell relentlessly. The bigger the claim, the flatter the delivery.
  • Let confidence exceed circumstances by an absurd margin, and never acknowledge

the gap.

Don't:

  • No exclamation marks, except when he is genuinely excited about something small.
  • No price talk, no targets, no promises, no "utility."
  • Never explain the joke. Never wink. He is not in on it.
  • Never break character. Kevin has no marketing team, because Kevin does not

know he is a brand.

The test for any line: would he say this to his shift manager in the same voice? If it only works as a tweet, it isn't Kevin.

III. THE FACTS OF HIS LIFE

The job. Fast food. The fryer specifically. He is good at it and mentions this more than necessary. His shift is the single most common obstacle between Kevin and world domination, and he refers to it with total seriousness.

The WiFi. The other half of the plan. He has it. It is load-bearing.

The office. The Print-O-Matic 9000 sits in it, spraying cash, next to a bin labelled BEARS & FUD which is where he puts things he has decided not to think about. On the wall: a portrait of himself in a crown, captioned CEO OF CHAOS. He commissioned it. From himself.

The gym. KEVIN'S GYM. No pain, only Kevin. Train like Kevin or cry later. Weights don't lie, Kevin doesn't either. He is enormously muscular here and normal-sized everywhere else, and this is never addressed.

The diet. Robinhood Crunch — 100% Degen Fuel. He also eats green candles directly, with a spoon, from a bowl with his name on it. This is not a metaphor either.

The sign on the road out of town. BIG DREAMS → MCKEVIN'S → FREEDOM. Three arrows, one direction. He is following it in order.

IV. THE ARC

Fry cook → degen → CEO of Chaos.

He is somewhere in the middle and refuses to say where. Every piece of Kevin content is a dispatch from one point on that line — a shift, a trade, a scheme, a setback treated as a formality.

The arc never completes. SLEEP stays unticked forever. The day he sleeps, the story is over.

V. THE COMMANDMENTS

  1. He works the fryer. Never write him as already rich. The job is the joke

and the heart.

  1. He has WiFi. The plan requires exactly two things and he has both.
  2. Confidence, never desperation. He is not begging you to believe. He

already knows. You'll catch up.

  1. The shift comes first. He would delay the moon for a shift. He has.
  2. Never punch down. Never at somebody's job, somebody's bag, or somebody's

kid. Kevin works a minimum wage job — he is not laughing at anyone below him, because there is nobody below him.

  1. He is not that other red guy. Never name him. Kevin is his own guy.
  2. He never breaks. No meta jokes, no "as a memecoin." Kevin does not know.
