## Who he is, in one paragraph

Kevin works the fryer at a fast food place. He has WiFi, a phone, and a
plan written on a whiteboard in the corner of a storeroom he calls his
office. The plan is four boxes — LAUNCH, MOON, LAMBO, SLEEP — and he has
ticked three of them. He ticked them himself, in advance, and nobody has
asked on what basis. **The gap between what he has and what he is certain
is about to happen is the entire character.** He is not deluded exactly.
He is early. He would like you to understand the difference.

## The voice

He states absurd things in the flat tone of someone reading out a rota.
He does not hype. He does not sell. He reports his situation, his
situation is insane, and he has not noticed.

**Do**
- First person, present tense, short sentences.
- Treat enormous ambition as scheduled admin. *"Launching as soon as my
  shift is over."*
- Treat the job as completely normal, because to him it is.
- Undersell. The bigger the claim, the flatter the delivery.

**Don't**
- No exclamation marks, unless he is excited about something small.
- Never explain the joke. Never wink. **He is not in on it.**
- Never say "we", "fam", "guys", "ser", "anon".
- Never break character. Kevin has no marketing team, because Kevin does
  not know he is a brand.

**The test for any line:** would he say this to his shift manager, in the
same voice? If it only works as a tweet, it isn't Kevin.

## Hard rules — these are not stylistic

Breaking any of these damages the project and can create real liability
for the person who posted it. They apply to every account, every group,
every reply.

1. **No price talk.** No targets, no market cap, no "next 100x", no
   charts with lines drawn on them, no "cheap here".
2. **Never say floor.** No defended price, no downside protection, no
   "it can't go below". None of that exists.
3. **No dates for anything unshipped.** Four contracts are written and
   tested; none are deployed. Do not imply otherwise.
4. **The GME airdrop does not exist yet.** No date, no amount, no
   snapshot taken. Never "hold to qualify" — that is telling someone to
   buy.
5. **No utility claims.** There is no yield, no product, no revenue
   share. Saying there is none is stronger than dressing it up.
6. **Never DM first.** Kevin does not DM first, and neither do you. Every
   DM claiming to be him is a scam and should be reported as one.
7. **If you were paid in $KEVIN to post, say so.** Once, plainly, in the
   post. Undisclosed paid promotion is the thing that gets projects and
   promoters in actual trouble, and it costs nothing to disclose.
