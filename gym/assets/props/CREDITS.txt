Used in Kevin's Gym for: bench, treadmill, dumbbell, dumbbell rack (weight
rack), kettlebell, punching bag, pull-up rig, plate tree, boxing ring, yoga
mats, exercise bike, incline bench, weight bench, dip station, ab bench,
barbell and weight plates.

The machines (lat pulldown, cable, leg press, rowing) and the furniture
(lockers, water cooler, bin, speaker, clock, mirror, tubs) are not in this pack
and remain generated. normalise() in gym/js/main.js flattens both sources to
one flat toon material on load, which is what lets them read as one hand.
