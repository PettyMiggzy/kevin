The kit ships GLB with the arcade atlas left EXTERNAL ("Textures/colormap.png"
beside the file), which gltf-transform cannot even inspect. Those five were
re-read so the loader resolved the image, had their texture URI cleared so it
embeds in the binary chunk, and were rewritten self-contained. Worth knowing
before ingesting more of that kit.

Kenney's palette is not Kevin's, and the crib looked like a furniture catalogue
until it was. The whole kit runs on eleven flat colours, so gym/js/brand.js maps
those eleven to the brand palette at load — the models are unmodified on disk.

AUTHORED, NOT DOWNLOADED

Six pieces no furniture or arcade kit ships: the card table, the chandelier, the
roulette wheel, the poker chips, the playing cards and the picture frame. Built
as geometry rather than sourced, because for shapes this simple authoring beat
downloading — licence-free, exactly on-grid, and a number in the script resizes
them.

They arrive with POSITION and COLOR_0 and NO material and NO normals. Both
matter: without normals the toon ramp has no surface direction to sample and
OutlineEffect cannot offset its hull, so they render as flat black silhouettes;
without vertexColors enabled their entire palette is discarded. normalise() in
gym/js/main.js handles both cases now. Anything authored the same way will need
the same treatment.
